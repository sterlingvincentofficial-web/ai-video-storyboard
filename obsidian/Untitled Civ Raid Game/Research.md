# Research

Full reports are in the repo's `reports/` folder: *Escape the Walls Roblox game*, *Indie games to adapt for Roblox*, *More indie games for Roblox*. Figures come from web-search summaries (from October 2026), not direct page reads.

## Key facts for this game
- **Roblox discovery (since about June 15, 2026):** ranks games on **28-day retention** (day 1, days 2–7, days 8–28) instead of 7-day. It also punishes thumbnails that promise more than the game delivers.
- **Steal-and-defend works:** Steal a Brainrot reportedly peaked around 20–24M players at once. Grow a Garden peaked around 22M, and 99 Nights in the Forest (made by a 3-person team) around 14M.
- **Straight copies of Steam games plateau** around 50K–84K players at once. The 1M+ hits are Roblox-native loops with long progression.
- **Survival and simulation** were the biggest and fastest-growing genres on Roblox in October 2026.
- **Earnings fade fast once a trend dies:** Grow a Garden went from about $12M in a month (May 2025) to an estimated $0.5–1.2M a month (2026).
