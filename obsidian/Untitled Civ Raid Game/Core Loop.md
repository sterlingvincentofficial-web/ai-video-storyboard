# Core Loop
Back to [[00 Home]]

```
Civilization makes money/sec
        ↓
Spend money: troops, gear, buildings, builders
        ↓
Send army to raid a ruler's camp  ──(while it marches, manage your civ)
        ↓
Troop grabs the loot → ruler wakes → waves chase you home
        ↓
Loot reaches your plot → boosts your civilization
        ↓
Civilization grows → more money/sec → harder rulers → next Age
```

## Two things to do at once
The game's best idea is that **raids and city-building happen at the same time**:
- Send troops, then walk with them and command them in [[Battle System]].
- Or let them march and go run your city: build a hospital, houses, morale buildings. See [[Civilization]].

**The base is the main part of the game.** You're always at your city unless you choose to go watch a raid. Raids run in the background while you build, so there's always something to do and nobody plays one round and logs off.

## Loops by time scale
| Time | What the player does |
|---|---|
| Seconds | Watch money tick up, command troops, retreat or redirect |
| One raid (about 2–4 min) | March, grab, survive the waves, get home |
| One session | Several raids, new buildings, gear upgrades, a new ruler unlocked |
| Daily | Collect offline income (capped), daily raid targets, rulers' loot respawns |
| Long term | Advance through [[Ages]], collect every ruler's treasure, fill the Museum |

## Why this fits Roblox's algorithm
Since June 2026, Roblox's recommendations rank games on **28-day retention** (day 1, days 2–7, days 8–28). That rewards long progression ([[Ages]], collections) and a reason to come back daily (capped offline income, respawning loot). See [[Research]].
