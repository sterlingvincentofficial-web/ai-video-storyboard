# Legendary Rulers
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Battle System]], [[Loot Ideas]], [[Ages]]

## Rules for picking rulers
- **No present-day or 20th-century political figures.** Nobody living, nobody from WWII or the Cold War. Roblox moderation treats real politics as sensitive, and it's not the game's vibe.
- **No religious figures.**
- **Respectful cultural depictions**: play up the funny side (Napoleon napping, Caesar posing) without stereotyping cultures.
- Legendary or semi-legendary figures (King Arthur) are fine.
- A **fictional ruler** works anywhere a real one is a bad fit.

## Starter roster
| Ruler | Camp | Fighting style | Signature loot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Napoleon | Army camp with tents and cannons | Cannons, cavalry chasers | Bicorne hat |
| Cleopatra | Riverside palace | Chariots, snake traps | Crown |
| Julius Caesar | Roman fort | Shield-wall legionaries | Laurel wreath |
| Genghis Khan | Steppe yurts | Fast horse archers that punish slow armies | Golden saddle |
| King Arthur | Castle | High-HP knights | Excalibur |
| A Viking king | Longhouse by the fjord | Berserkers, longship landings | Horned helm (fun, not historical) |
| Ramses II | Pyramid / temple | Sun-themed waves | Golden mask |
| Leonidas | Mountain pass | Few but very strong defenders | Spartan shield |
| Queen Elizabeth I | Palace | Royal guards | Royal jewels |

## Each ruler should have
- A **sleeping or idle animation** at the start of a raid (the "wake up" moment is the clip people share).
- A **wake-up reaction** with a voice line or sound.
- Their own wave style and a final **boss wave** where they fight personally.
