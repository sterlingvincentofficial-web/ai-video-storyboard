# Civilization
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Ages]], [[Spending and Upgrades]]

**Your base is the main part of the game.** You're always at your city running it, unless you choose to go watch an attack.

## Money per second
```
money/sec = workers × building output × morale × age bonus
```

## Needs
| Need | If it's ignored |
|---|---|
| **Housing** | Population stops growing |
| **Food** | People get sick and output drops |
| **Health** | Sickness spreads; sick workers stop working |
| **Morale** | The money multiplier drops |
| **Work** | The money-makers themselves |

## Builders
Nothing builds itself. **Hire builders**; more builders means more builds at once.

## How buildings work
- Every need has a **category**.
- **Every Age has its own buildings in each category**, based on what people really did in that era.
- **Each Age has several different buildings per category**, not one. Each building has its own levels.
- **You can't get a better building without advancing the Age.**
- When you advance, every placed building becomes **level 1 of its next-Age version** (see [[Ages]]).

It keeps coming in while you're offline but **caps after a few hours**, so players come back to collect.
