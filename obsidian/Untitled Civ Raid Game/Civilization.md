# Civilization
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Ages]], [[Spending and Upgrades]]

Your plot is **your own little country**. Its citizens and buildings are what make your **money per second**.

## Money per second
```
money/sec = workers × building output × morale × age bonus × treasure bonuses
```
- **Workers**: citizens who have a job in a building.
- **Building output**: what each workplace produces; upgrading raises it.
- **Morale**: a multiplier. Happy people work harder (for example 0.5× to 1.5×).
- **Age bonus**: each new [[Ages|Age]] multiplies everything.
- **Treasure bonuses**: stolen loot on display adds a boost (see [[Loot Ideas]]).

## Citizens' needs (keep it to four)
| Need | Building that covers it | What happens if it's ignored |
|---|---|---|
| **Housing** | Homes | Population stops growing |
| **Food** | Farms, then markets | People get sick and output drops |
| **Health** | Doctor's building / hospital | Sickness spreads and sick workers stop working |
| **Morale** | Tavern, theater, park, statues, festivals | The morale multiplier drops |

The point of the needs: while your troops are out raiding, there's always something worth fixing at home.

## Builders
- **Nothing builds itself.** You **hire builders** to construct and upgrade buildings.
- More builders means more builds at the same time; builder upgrades make them faster.
- Builders are visible little people walking around your plot, hammering. Watching them is part of the fun.

## Building list (first draft)
| Building | Does |
|---|---|
| Homes | Raise the population cap |
| Farm / Fishery | Food |
| Workshop / Mine / Market | Main money-makers (workplaces) |
| Doctor's building, then hospital | Cures sickness |
| Tavern / Theater / Park | Morale |
| Barracks | Trains troops and unlocks army slots |
| Armory | Sells and upgrades troop gear (see [[Spending and Upgrades]]) |
| Builder's Hut | Hire more builders |
| Treasury / Museum | Shows off stolen loot and gives its bonuses |
| Academy | Research to unlock the next [[Ages|Age]] |
| Watchtower (if PvP) | Defense; see [[Open Questions]] |

## Offline income
Money keeps coming in while you're offline, but **caps after a few hours**, so players come back to collect it. That helps day-1 and day-7 retention (see [[Research]]).
