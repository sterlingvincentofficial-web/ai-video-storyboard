# Ages
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Civilization]], [[Spending and Upgrades]]

## The rule: advancing an Age resets nothing
- You **don't need to be maxed out** to advance. It's just hard if you're not.
- When you advance, **every building you've unlocked and placed upgrades to level 1 of its next-Age version.**
  - Example: Campfire L5 → Wrestling Ring L1. Healer's Hut L3 → Physician's House L1. Level 2 huts → level 1 mud-brick houses.
- **Better buildings are only available in a new Age.** Within an Age you have multiple buildings per category, each with levels (see [[Civilization]]).
- **Troops are not upgraded by Ages.** They keep their level and upgrade separately through the War Tent tree (see [[Spending and Upgrades]]).
- **Robux items are never replaced.**

## Balance rule (important)
A building at **level 1 of the next Age must earn more than the max level of the previous Age.** Otherwise advancing early would lower your money per second. Players should never feel punished for advancing.

## Each building needs a successor
Every building in an Age points to exactly **one building in the next Age**, so the upgrade knows what to turn into. If the next Age has more buildings in a category than the last one, the extras are new unlocks bought normally.
