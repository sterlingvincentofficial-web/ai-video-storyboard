# Ages
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Civilization]], [[Legendary Rulers]]

Advancing an Age is the big progression step, like a rebirth that **visibly transforms your whole plot**.

| Age | Your plot looks like | Troops | Rulers you can raid (ideas) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stone Age | Huts, campfires | Clubs, slings | A tribal chief (fictional) |
| Bronze Age / Ancient | Mud-brick, temples | Spears, bronze swords | Ramses II, Cleopatra |
| Classical | Marble, columns | Legionaries, archers | Julius Caesar, Alexander the Great, Leonidas |
| Medieval | Castles, stone walls | Knights, crossbows | King Arthur, Charlemagne, a Viking king, Genghis Khan |
| Renaissance / Gunpowder | Towns, cathedrals | Musketeers, cannons | Queen Elizabeth I, a shogun |
| Napoleonic / Industrial | Factories, trains | Rifles, cavalry | Napoleon |
| Later Ages (to discuss) | Cities, then space | Up to the team | Fictional or futuristic bosses only, no 20th-century leaders (see [[Legendary Rulers]]) |

## Advancing
- Needs Academy research plus a set of goals (population, money/sec, rulers raided).
- **Keeps:** relics, unlocked troop types, a permanent multiplier.
- **Resets (to discuss):** building levels and money, like a classic rebirth. Or nothing resets and it's purely an upgrade. See [[Open Questions]].
