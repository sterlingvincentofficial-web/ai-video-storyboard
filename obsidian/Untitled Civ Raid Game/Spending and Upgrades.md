# Spending and Upgrades (what money buys)
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Battle System]], [[Civilization]]

Spending is the core loop: **money your civilization earns buys things that change raids and grow your city.**

## Troop gear (assigned to individual troops)
| Item | Effect | Why it matters in raids |
|---|---|---|
| **Shoes / boots** | Movement speed | Outrun the chasers and skip later waves |
| **Armor / helmets** | More HP | Carrier survives longer |
| **Shields** | Block damage | Tank troops that protect the carrier |
| **Weapons** | More damage | Win wave fights faster |
| **Backpack / cart** | Carry bigger loot, or the carrier runs faster while carrying | Lets you steal heavier treasures |
| **Banner** | Aura buff to nearby troops | Stronger formations |
| **Mounts** (horses, camels; unlocked by Age) | Big speed boost | Late-game way to escape |
| **Medic kit** | Revive a knocked-down troop once per raid | Comebacks |

Gear **upgrades through tiers per Age**: wooden club → bronze sword → steel sword → musket → and so on.

## Troop types
| Type | Role |
|---|---|
| **Carrier / Runner** | Fast; the best one to grab the loot |
| **Swordsman** | All-round fighter |
| **Shieldbearer** | Tank that blocks for the carrier |
| **Archer** | Damage from range |
| **Medic** | Heals and revives knocked-down troops |
| **Scout** | Shows which waves are coming and the route home |
| **Engineer** | Opens locked gates, disables traps at camps |
| **Cavalry** (later Ages) | Fast and strong, expensive |

## Army upgrades
- **More army slots** (bought at the Barracks).
- **Training levels** per troop.
- **Formations** you unlock: wedge, box around the carrier, spread.

## Battle consumables (one-time use)
- **War drums**: everyone runs faster for 10 s.
- **Smoke bomb**: the chasing wave loses track of you for a few seconds.
- **Bandages**: quick heal.
- **Decoy loot**: makes a wave chase the wrong troop.

## Civilization spending
- Buildings and building upgrades (see [[Civilization]])
- **Hiring builders**
- Research at the Academy to advance [[Ages]]
- Decorations and statues (morale)
- Unlocking new map zones and rulers (see [[Legendary Rulers]])

## Robux (later, to discuss)
The earned-money loop above is the core. What, if anything, is sold for Robux is still open (see [[Open Questions]]).
