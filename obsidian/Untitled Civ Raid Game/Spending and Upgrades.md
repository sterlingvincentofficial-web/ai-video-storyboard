# Spending and Upgrades
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Battle System]], [[Civilization]], [[Loot Ideas]]

Money your civilization earns buys gear, battle items, troop upgrades and buildings.

## Troop types
| Type | Role |
|---|---|
| **Fighters** | The core of the army; most units hatched from eggs |
| **Carriers** | Any troop can carry an egg, but carriers are **better at it** (faster while carrying, tougher) and are **always first to grab the egg** |
| **Medics** | Heal troops and revive knocked-down ones |
| **Commanders** | While a commander is **alive**, nearby troops get a **damage and speed boost**. If the commander goes down, the boost is gone, so the army has to protect them. |

Commander ideas: each commander has one **active ability** the player can trigger, for example a Charge (army sprints for 5 s) or a Rally (everyone heals 20%).

## Troop upgrade tree (separate from Ages)
- **Troops never stop upgrading.** Advancing an [[Ages|Age]] does **not** upgrade troops automatically; they stay at their level until you upgrade them.
- Upgrades happen at the **War Tent** (working name) as a **tech tree** with branches:
  - **Attack** (damage)
  - **Defense** (HP and armor)
  - **Speed** (march and escape speed)
  - **Carrying** (carry speed, egg protection)
  - **Support** (medic healing, commander aura strength)
- Individual troops also level up.
- To decide: whether new Ages unlock new branches or new tiers of the tree. See [[Open Questions]].

## Troop gear
Shoes (speed, so fast troops can **outrun later waves**), armor, shields, weapons, backpacks or carts for carriers, banners (buff nearby troops), and horses or camels (unlocked later). Gear looks different in each Age.

## Battle items (one-time use, bought with money)
Designed around the raid's three phases: **the grab, the fight at camp, and the chase home**.

### Before the alarm (the grab)
- **Sleeping Draught**: the ruler stays asleep longer, giving extra seconds before wave 1. It gets stronger in later eras (a lullaby, then sleeping powder, then a music box).
- **Quiet Boots**: your carrier grabs the egg without waking the camp straight away.

### Fighting at camp
- **Rally Horn**: knocked-down troops get back up at 30% HP. Once per raid.
- **Commander's Banner**: plant it and troops near it hit harder for 15 s.
- **Field Barrage**: your home sends one strike on the enemy wave. It scales by era: rocks, then catapult, then cannon, then artillery.

### The chase home
- **Barricade**: drop it behind your army and it blocks the chasing wave for a few seconds. Log pile → palisade → stone wall → sandbags.
- **Caltrops / Oil Slick**: the chasing wave runs slower through a patch.
- **Decoy Egg**: a fake egg on a second runner. Half the wave chases the wrong one.
- **Bribe Chest**: pay gold and one wave turns back. A big money sink for rich players.
- **Signal Flare**: reserve troops from your plot run out to meet your army on the way home.
- **Shortcut Map**: unlocks a faster route home for this raid.
- **Egg Cradle**: the egg doesn't drop the first time its carrier goes down.

### Intel
- **Spyglass**: see the next wave's units before it's released, so you know whether to run or fight.

Items should **look and sound different per era**, so they feel new after every Age.

## Robux
- **Robux items are permanent.** A Robux unit, for example a Mega Commander, is **never replaced** by anything, including Age upgrades.
- To decide: do Robux units level up through the tree like normal troops? See [[Open Questions]].
