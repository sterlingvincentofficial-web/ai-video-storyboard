# Battle System
Back to [[00 Home]] · Related: [[Spending and Upgrades]], [[Legendary Rulers]]

**Goal for the feel:** action-packed and suspenseful, not too fast and not too slow. The player is the **commander**; troops do the fighting and carrying.

## Flow of one raid
1. **Army slots.** Troops you assign to army slots line up in formation at the **front of your plot**, so everyone can see your army.
2. **Send Troops.** Click it and the **world map** opens. Pick a zone, like Napoleon's Camp. Each zone shows its difficulty, number of waves, and the loot you'd get.
3. **March.** Troops walk across the real map. You can **walk along and follow them**, or stay home and manage your civilization.
4. **Commands during the march:**
   - **Retreat**: everyone heads home, at any time.
   - **Change target**: send them to a different zone.
   - Later ideas: *Hold position*, *Charge*, *Protect the carrier*.
5. **Arrival.** The ruler is asleep, for example Napoleon dozing on his chair, and so are his guards. One of your troops **picks up the loot**.
6. **Alarm.** The ruler and guards **wake up**, and **Wave 1** fights your troops right there.
7. **Escape.** Win wave 1 and your troops **run home with the loot**. The ruler then **sends wave 2 after you**, and wave 3 if there is one.
8. **Home.** The loot crosses into your plot and is safe. It goes to your Treasury or Museum (see [[Loot Ideas]]).

## Health and getting knocked out
- Every troop, guard and ruler has a **health bar**.
- A troop that hits 0 HP is **knocked down** and lies on the ground until the battle ends, then **teleports back to your plot**. Nobody dies permanently; we lose time, not troops.
- **If the carrier goes down**, the loot drops and another of your troops can pick it up. This is a big source of suspense.
- **If every troop goes down**, the raid fails and the loot goes back to the ruler.

## Gear changes the fight (see [[Spending and Upgrades]])
- **Shoes** (speed): fast troops can outrun the chasers and **skip later waves** entirely.
- Armor keeps the carrier alive; weapons win wave fights faster; medics revive knocked-down troops.
- So money earned at home directly changes how raids play.

## What makes it suspenseful
- **A distance-home bar** with the next chasing wave shown behind you on it.
- **The carrier's health bar** shown big on screen.
- **A wave warning**: drums and a red edge on the screen when the next wave is released.
- **The ruler as boss of the last wave**: Napoleon himself rides out on wave 3.
- **Choices mid-raid**: keep running, or turn and fight the wave? Retreat and lose the loot, or risk it?

## Pacing targets (tune in playtests)
| Part | Target length |
|---|---|
| March to the camp | 30–60 s (longer for far zones) |
| Wave 1 at the camp | 30–45 s |
| Escape with waves 2 and 3 | 60–90 s |
| **Whole raid** | **about 2–4 minutes** |

## Ruler behaviour ideas
- Each ruler fights differently: Napoleon fires cannons and sends cavalry; Cleopatra sends chariots; Genghis Khan sends fast horse archers that punish slow armies; King Arthur has knights with high HP.
- Rulers' camps **respawn their loot on a timer**, which gives a reason to come back.
- After you raid a ruler, their next waves get a bit tougher, so they keep up with the player's power.
