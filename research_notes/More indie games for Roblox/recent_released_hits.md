# Recent released indie hits (June–Oct 2026) as candidates for original Roblox games

Method note: I could only use web search results. WebFetch failed (DNS) and curl got 403 from the proxy policy for Steam, SteamDB, Roblox (games.roblox.com), GameDiscoverCo, GamingOnLinux, CBC and others. So I didn't open any page in full, and the Roblox search API couldn't be reached. Every figure below comes from search-result summaries of the cited URL. The search budget (200 per turn) ran out before I finished, and the Gaps sections list what was left unchecked. Already-covered games were skipped: Meccha Chameleon, How to Fish, Bombanana!, Dressmaker, Invokyr, WARDOGS, Scam With Your Friends, PEAK, Keep It Up!, Dear Passengers, and the rest of the exclusion list.

## Key Question 1: Which recent released indie hits qualify (name, developer, app ID, date, success evidence)?

### Takeaway
I found 13 games released between June and October 2026 that showed hit signals and had no direct Roblox version: Big Walk, Shift At Midnight, Cat Mail Co., ReStory, Iron Nest, over the hill, Witchspire, WheelMates, Funnel Runners, Sandustry, Solarpunk, Voidling Bound and Pass the Fear. Two more hits, Machine Party and Burglin' Gnomes, are rejected because Roblox already has a game with the same name or the same loop. The strongest are Big Walk (1M copies in 6 days), Shift At Midnight (953k copies in July), ReStory (500k+), Iron Nest (~650k) and Cat Mail Co. (236k, cozy).

## Key Question 2: Core mechanic, why players and streamers love it, and Roblox fit (1–5)

### Takeaway
The best Roblox fits are Cat Mail Co. (5), Big Walk (4–5), ReStory (4), over the hill (4), Witchspire (4), WheelMates (4) and Shift At Midnight (4, after toning down the horror). Iron Nest, Sandustry and Voidling Bound fit worse because they are single-player, depend on simulation depth, or their mechanic wasn't confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Big Walk:** online co-op for 2–12 players in an open bushland world with puzzles, proximity chat and items for talking to each other. The map is based on Wilsons Promontory National Park — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Walk); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/big-walk-hits-1m-sales-in-6-days-outpacing-goose-game). **Fit 4–5:** social, built on proximity chat, big lobbies, simple walking and puzzles. Roblox voice chat is spatial but limited to age-verified users 13+ — [search summary of Roblox voice info](https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/news/create-a-roblox-voice-chat). So a Roblox version needs text or emote signalling tools, which would echo Big Walk's communication items.
- **Shift At Midnight:** you run a gas station and question each customer, because some are doppelgangers. One that slips past comes back later in the shift. It plays like Papers, Please with co-op — [search summary of Xbox/Bloody Disgusting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3960583/clock-into-co-op-detective-horror-title-shift-at-midnight-now-on-pc-xbox-trailer/); [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/july-2026s-top-new-pc-and-console). Streamers like the deduction and jump-scare moments, and CaseOh and Markiplier played the demo — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/shift-at-midnight-how-the-breakout-hit-dominated-the-steam-charts/). **Fit 4 (mature flag):** rated 16+, so a Roblox version needs cartoony "impostor" tells instead of gore.
- **Cat Mail Co.:** a cozy cat post office where you sort, weigh, stamp and deliver parcels, with no timers or penalties and 4-player co-op — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/cozy-post-office-sim-cat-mail-co-is-pawsitively-ready-to-ship-out-on-july-6th-). **Fit 5:** young audience, cute animals, short job loops, easy upgrade and decoration progression.
- **ReStory:** run a small repair shop in mid-2000s Tokyo. You take in Y2K gadgets, diagnose, clean, solder, replace parts and reassemble them. Includes licensed Atari consoles and multiple endings — [search summary of KeenGamer/SteamDeckHQ](https://www.keengamer.com/games/restory-chill-electronics-repairs/). **Fit 4:** a satisfying job-sim loop that maps to a repair-shop tycoon. It's single-player, so multiplayer has to be designed in, for example as a co-op shop.
- **Iron Nest:** a first-person dieselpunk artillery sim. You plot coordinates and load and fire the guns of a 5,000-ton spider turret by hand — [Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/games/pc/iron-nest-heavy-turret-simulator); [Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/24570/steam-overwhelmingly-positive-with-99-rating-dieselpunk-turret-simulator-iron-nest). **Fit 3 (war theme):** the hands-on "crew a giant machine" fantasy could become a co-op crew game, one player per station, aimed at non-human targets. Ballistics UI is harder for young players.
- **over the hill:** chill 1–4 player co-op off-road exploration from the art of rally studio — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/-over-the-hill-arrives-on-steam-october-14). **Fit 4:** vehicles plus exploring with friends suits Roblox, and you can unlock vehicles and add-ons.
- **Witchspire:** a magic-themed cozy open-world co-op sandbox with crafting — [Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/22703/magic-open-world-adventure-witchspire-launches-in-pc-early-access); [search summary](https://www.analogstickgaming.com/news/2025/12/4/magical-open-world-adventure-witchspire-reveals-first-gameplay). **Fit 4:** witches, brooms, spells and collecting all suit young players.
- **WheelMates:** two players drive tiny RC cars through a missing professor's maze-like lab-mansion. Puzzles need both players, and gadgets include rope swings, magnetic wheels and grapples. No timers — [Co-Optimus](https://www.co-optimus.com/article/17871/wheelmates-the-co-op-rc-car-driving-game-races-onto-steam.html); [TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/01/28/wheelmates-is-a-new-co-op-game-where-you-play-as-remote-control-cars/). **Fit 4:** a duo obby built around RC cars fits Roblox well.
- **Funnel Runners:** first-person co-op for 1–8 players. You scavenge parts to repair an escape van while tornadoes grow stronger — [search summary](https://www.timeextension.com/games/pc/funnel_runners). **Fit 3:** the theme works, but the genre is crowded (see Key Question 3).
- **Sandustry:** 2D falling-sand-physics mining and factory automation, "Factorio meets Terraria" — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/sandustry/steam-hooded-horse). **Fit 2:** per-pixel sand simulation is costly on Roblox, and the audience skews toward automation fans.
- **Pass the Fear:** 4-player co-op roguelite bullet-hell — [PlayCo-opGame](https://playco-opgame.com/pass-the-fear/). **Fit 3:** a short-run roguelite works on Roblox, but the theme wasn't checked.
- **Solarpunk / Voidling Bound:** co-op open-world survival, cozy per the Game Pass headline — [search summary](https://www.notices.mce.ca.gov/2026/06/xbox-game-pass-adds-super-cozy-day-one-open-world-game-with-co-op/). Voidling Bound's mechanic wasn't verified. Fit not assessed.

### Inferences
- The most Roblox-ready pattern is a cozy job sim (post office, repair shop) or a social co-op traversal game (Big Walk, RC cars, off-road), because each splits into 5–15 minute loops with cosmetic and upgrade progression.
- Doppelganger-checking (Shift At Midnight) has proven streamer appeal, but the genre already exists on Roblox, so an original take needs a fresh setting.

### Gaps
- No Twitch or YouTube viewership numbers per title (TwitchTracker and SullyGnome couldn't be fetched) except Big Walk's mention in Twitch's yearly report.
- The mechanics of Voidling Bound and Solarpunk weren't confirmed because the search budget ran out.

## Key Question 4: Rejected (Roblox version already exists or loop too close) and branding to avoid

### Takeaway
Reject Machine Party (a Roblox experience with the same name exists) and Burglin' Gnomes (Roblox "Gnomes!" uses the same sneak-in, loot, escape-the-homeowner loop).
