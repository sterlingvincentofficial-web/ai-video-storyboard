## Which new upcoming Steam indies (Next Fest Oct 2026 / Popular Upcoming / wishlist charts) are viable, and what is the virality evidence?

### Takeaway
Steam Next Fest October 2026 (Oct 19–26) was dominated by co-op "friendslop" party games. Beyond the already-covered top titles, Valve's top 20 most-played list includes several Roblox-friendly physics and co-op concepts: Dumpster Gang (#12), HANDS OVER (#13), No Body No Problem! (#16), Grr Grr Gnomes (#19) and Abyssal Lapidary (#10). Editorial picks with clippable hooks include Fallbearers (Kotaku's top pick), My Arms Are Longer Now (Jackbox-published) and Meltdown Crew (~2.4K peak). The strongest wide-open picks, with no Roblox version or close genre found, are **Fallbearers**, **Dumpster Gang** and **My Arms Are Longer Now**.

### Inferences
- **Roblox fit ratings** (young audience / multiplayer / short sessions / simple build / progression room):
  - **Fallbearers – 5/5.** Two-player physics carry is cheap to build. Each run is a 2–5 minute obby-like course, and failures are inherently funny and clippable. Loops could include unlockable coffin contents, courses and cosmetics. The funeral theme should be softened (e.g., carrying a sleeping king, a giant cake or a pet) for a kid audience.
  - **Dumpster Gang – 5/5.** Animal mischief co-op suits a young audience. Short heist levels, collectible junk and a "trash hoard" collection or rebirth loop map well to Roblox simulators.
  - **My Arms Are Longer Now – 4/5.** The stretch-arm grab is a highly clippable verb, and a "grab loot from far away without guards seeing" loop is easy to make multiplayer, e.g. as a heist round. 2D-to-3D translation adds some build complexity.
  - **Meltdown Crew – 4/5.** A panic-management co-op with phones and proximity chat makes great clip material. However, Roblox voice chat is age-gated, so the communication gimmick needs a text or phone-UI substitute. Roblox reactor games are an established niche (Pinewood-style), so differentiation must come from the co-op chaos.
  - **Grr Grr Gnomes – 4/5.** Small characters in a big world, plus sailing and scavenging, fits Roblox well. Raft survival is already crowded.
  - **DYST: Did You See That? – 4/5.** A two-player "what do you see?" mechanic fits anomaly-game popularity on Roblox and pushes friends to talk to each other. Horror should be kept mild.
  - **Arctic Drive – 4/5.** The "upgrade your vehicle while surviving" loop has proven itself in A Dusty Trip. It is differentiable only by setting.
  - **The Peg Mines – 4/5.** Incremental and pachinko mechanics fit Roblox progression perfectly, but virality evidence is weak.
  - **HANDS OVER – 3/5.** Luck, bluffing and elimination rounds fit Roblox party games ("Pass or Die"-style). Body-horror amputation must be removed for younger players.
  - **No Body No Problem! / We Are So Cooked – 3/5.** Asymmetric cop-vs-suspects is a strong round-based format, but the "hide the corpse" theme needs reskinning for Roblox (e.g., "hide the broken vase before Mom gets home").
  - **Abyssal Lapidary – 3/5.** It is a solo shop sim, and Roblox gem tycoons already exist.
- **Distinctive branding that must NOT be reused:**
  - Fallbearers: the title, Victorian pallbearer characters, the "Coffin Flop" framing, the bulldog.
  - Dumpster Gang: the title, raccoon gang characters, the exterminator antagonist.
  - My Arms Are Longer Now: the title, the long-armed thief protagonist, the near-retirement detective, the sock-puppet flirting gag, Jackbox/Toot branding.
  - HANDS OVER: the title, its specific cursed childhood toys, influence-card designs, ghost mechanic naming.
  - No Body No Problem! and We Are So Cooked: the titles and their characters.
  - Meltdown Crew: the title, 1980s reactor UI and phones.
  - Grr Grr Gnomes: the title, gnome characters.
  - DYST: the title, carnival setting.
  - Arctic Drive: the title.
  - The Peg Mines: the title, crafted-ball deck art.
  - Abyssal Lapidary: the title, shop characters.
  - Art styles were not visible, since the pages could not be fetched.
- The most open space (no direct clone or close genre found) is Fallbearers, Dumpster Gang and My Arms Are Longer Now. The others have an adjacent Roblox genre and would need a clear twist.

## Which candidates were rejected (Roblox version or saturated genre exists, already released, or poor fit)?

### Takeaway
Rejected titles: just volleyball (Roblox volleyball is saturated), Banana Airways (direct co-op airliner analogues already on Roblox), and Crashout Crew, Stream Train and Burglin' Gnomes (already released, so not "upcoming"). Large RPG/survival demos (Emberville, Wyldheart, Never Be Alone, ArcheAge Chronicles) and Character House were dropped as poor Roblox-clone fits.
