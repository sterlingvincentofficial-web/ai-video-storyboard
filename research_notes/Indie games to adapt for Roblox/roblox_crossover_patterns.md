# Indie/Steam → Roblox Crossover Patterns and the Late-2026 Roblox Trend Landscape

> Research method note (Oct 26, 2026): The proxy blocked direct page fetches for every target host tried (kotaku.com, windowscentral.com, about.roblox.com, studiokrew.com, and per proxy logs devforum.roblox.com and store.steampowered.com returned 403 / DNS failure). **All findings below come from web-search result summaries, not full-page reads.** Numbers are snapshots from different dates and trackers (RoWatcher, Rolimons, RoMonitor, LiveCounts, Statroblox, etc.) that often disagree. Treat them as approximate and check the live trackers before relying on them.

## Q1. Case studies: Roblox hits inspired by indie/Steam games (original → Roblox version, speed, visits/CCU, did it outgrow the original?)

### Takeaway
Since 2024, nearly every viral Steam co-op or indie hit has had Roblox versions within days or weeks. Several have matched or beaten the original on live players, though usually only when the player counts of several clones are added together. The clearest 2026 cases are Meccha Chameleon (26 clones in about 3 weeks, combined CCU above the original's) and Librarian (Clean the Library peaked at about 84K CCU while the Steam game had about 7K playing). The cycle has also sped up a lot: Escape the Walls (Oct 2026) had a Roblox copy within about 25 hours of being announced, before its own Steam page existed.

### Cited Findings

**Meccha Chameleon (Steam, 2026) → "[BACKROOMS] Chameleon!", "Paint Or Seek" + ~26 clones**
- Kotaku (July 15, 2026), using RoWatcher data: the original's all-time peak was just over 340,000 with about 100,000 playing at the time. The leading Roblox clone "[BACKROOMS] Chameleon!" had nearly 50,000 CCU and "Paint Or Seek" had 37,000. The combined Roblox clones overtook the Steam game's then-current player count — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/more-people-are-playing-meccha-chameleon-knock-offs-than-the-original-hit-steam-game-as-the-roblox-clone-machine-goes-into-overdrive-2000715371); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/roblox-clones-of-meccha-chameleon-have-more-players-than-the-original-steam-sensation-after-just-3-weeks/)
- Speed: about 26 clones appeared within roughly 3 weeks. By early July their combined peak CCU exceeded the original's — [Newzoo/GEEIQ via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-and-geeiq-the-roblox-audience)
- A later snapshot: clones at 150K–200K+ DAU, with the Steam original down to about 55K CCU. This is a different metric (DAU vs CCU), so it doesn't compare directly with Kotaku's figures — [Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-clones-meccha-chameleon-more-players/)
- The original reportedly sold 15 million copies in its first month. It costs $6 on Steam, while the Roblox versions are free — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898)
- Fortnite imitators also appeared — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898)
- The "[BACKROOMS]" prefix on the top clone shows creators stacking an established Roblox trend keyword onto the new concept — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/more-people-are-playing-meccha-chameleon-knock-offs-than-the-original-hit-steam-game-as-the-roblox-clone-machine-goes-into-overdrive-2000715371)

**Librarian: Tidy Up the Arcane Library (Steam, paid) → "Clean the Library" (Roblox)**

The Roblox version reportedly peaked at **83,591 CCU and about 70M visits in July 2026**. This one clearly outgrew the original — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898); also Kotaku coverage titled "Tidy up the Backrooms" — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/games/tidy-up-the-backrooms)

**Escape the Walls (Pandorio Games, announced Oct 14, 2026; incremental wall-punching game) → "Escape-the-Wall" + 7 or more clones**
- Pandorio announced the game on Oct 14. The Steam page was "coming soon," with no release date or price — [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/); [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/37989/viral-wall-punching-game-already-has-roblox-clones)
- A Roblox copy titled "Escape-the-Wall" went live Oct 15, the day after the announcement (another outlet says within 25 hours). At least 7 Roblox clones existed by Oct 19. At least one clone also appeared on Steam — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Thisisgame Thailand](https://thisisgamethailand.com/games/console-pc/pc/viral-game-escape-the-walls-copied-on-roblox-less-than-a-day/); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/)
- Some clone makers credited AI tools, including Claude, for building their games. They argued the concept was generic — [Ralify](https://ralify.net/stories/viral-indie-game-escape-the-walls-faces-immediate-roblox-copycat/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/)

### Inferences
- The "outgrew the original" claims hold mostly for **paid Steam games with simple, readable loops** (hide-and-seek with paint, cleaning a library, climbing). Roblox's free entry and its very large young audience mean that a clone with a fraction of Roblox's attention can still beat a $6–$20 Steam game on live CCU. When the top clone sits at 50K–84K CCU, it is a solid Roblox mid-tier hit, not a front-page mega-hit. Clones of Steam games rarely reach the 1M+ tier that original Roblox-native concepts (Dead Rails, 99 Nights, Grow a Garden) reach.
- The time to market has shrunk from weeks (Lethal Company, 2023–24) to 2 weeks (PEAK, 2025), then 3 weeks for 26 clones (Meccha Chameleon, 2026), and now less than 25 hours (Escape the Walls, Oct 2026). AI-assisted building is cited as the accelerator.
- Since 2025 the mega-hits have been Roblox-native concepts with a long loop (survival nights, grow/collect, steal-and-defend). Even games that borrow loosely from Steam (Dead Rails, 99 Nights) went far bigger than the direct clones.

### Gaps
- No verified CCU or visit data found for Roblox versions of **Schedule I, Content Warning, Only Up, Getting Over It, or Backrooms** games specifically. Searches returned nothing citable.
- Doors (Roblox-native, LSPLASH) is a reference point, but no current numbers were retrieved.
- CCU figures for E.R.P.O. and CLIMB could not be checked against a tracker, because fetches were blocked.
- No Escape the Walls clone performance numbers (visits/CCU) were found. Coverage only goes to Oct 19.

## Q2. Success factors and failure cases

### Takeaway
The ingredients that recur in Roblox hits are: being first while a Steam game is trending on YouTube/TikTok; being free on a social, co-op-friendly platform; borrowing a recognisable name or thumbnail plus a Roblox trend tag (e.g., "[BACKROOMS]"); adding Roblox-style monetisation (passes, cosmetics, pay-to-win shortcuts); and frequent updates. The failures are shallow clones that rely on thumbnails, which the June 2026 algorithm change now penalises, and clones that copy branding or assets and draw public backlash from the original developer, which can force a rename or shutdown (CLIFF → RISE).

### Cited Findings
- **Speed and riding the original's hype:** Escape the Walls had a copy within about 25 hours and 7 or more clones in 5 days. Meccha Chameleon had 26 clones in 3 weeks — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Newzoo/GEEIQ](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-and-geeiq-the-roblox-audience)
- **Price gap:** Paid Steam originals ($6 Meccha Chameleon, paid Librarian) against free Roblox entry — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898)
- **Influencers, distribution and algorithm:** These were credited as the boosters behind Dead Rails' breakout — [GameAnalytics](https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/dead-rails-and-the-hit-makers-formula)
- **Monetisation layer:** Clones add game passes, cosmetics and class unlocks — [NoPing](https://noping.com/blog/repo-clones-on-roblox). PEAK's developers called CLIFF "microtransaction-riddled" and said players could "buy a win" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/after-5-million-sales-dev-of-hit-underwater-soulslike-tells-people-to-just-pirate-its-new-viral-mounting-climbing-game-peak-instead-of-wasting-time-and-money-on-microtransaction-riddled-roblox-slop-ripoffs-where-you-can-buy-a-win/)
- **Open crediting:** Clean the Library credits Librarian on its page — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/roblox-adaptations-are-turning-steams-biggest-indie-hits-free-play-competitors-163898). CLIFF thanked PEAK's creators in its description, but still drew backlash — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/how-roblox-theft-is-becoming-a-big-problem)
- **Legal line:** A legal expert quoted by Kotaku says similar concepts or gameplay alone don't establish copyright infringement. Copying branding or specific assets is a different matter — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/more-people-are-playing-meccha-chameleon-knock-offs-than-the-original-hit-steam-game-as-the-roblox-clone-machine-goes-into-overdrive-2000715371)
- **Failure and backlash cases:** CLIFF was publicly attacked by Aggro Crab and Landfall, then renamed RISE with new graphics or shut down (reports conflict) — [Bounding Into Comics](https://boundingintocomics.com/video-games/video-game-news/peak-developers-call-roblox-game-rise-formerly-cliff-a-slop-ripoff-and-encourage-players-to-pirate-our-game-instead); [Eneba](https://www.eneba.com/hub/news/peak-dev-calls-out-roblox-slop-ripoff-cliff-clone-shuts-down/). Deadly Company was flagged as at risk of shutdown for being too close to the original, though such cases are rare — [Gamewave.fr](https://gamewave.fr/roblox/deadly-company-la-copie-de-lethal-company-qui-cartonne-sur-roblox/)
- **Reputation risk:** Mainstream outlets now frame clones as "AI slop" exploiting indie developers — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/how-roblox-theft-is-becoming-a-big-problem); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/)
- **Thumbnail tactics:** Roblox staff advise avoiding clickbait thumbnails that "lead to quick bounces." Thumbnail personalisation and A/B testing exist — [DevForum: 5 tips on thumbnail personalization](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/5-tips-from-roblox-staff-to-get-the-most-out-of-thumbnail-personalization/3471689); [DevForum: thumbnail personalization](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/get-your-thumbnails-ready-for-thumbnail-personalization/3226599)

### Inferences
- With so many clones (7 to 26 per trend), being first is no longer enough. The clones that win (e.g., "[BACKROOMS] Chameleon!" and Clean the Library) seem to combine the trending concept with an established Roblox genre or keyword and a long-term loop. The June 2026 move to 28-day retention means the cheap "quick clone" will probably rank worse over time unless it retains players.
- Safest legal and PR posture: borrow the *mechanic*, use original name, art and assets, and credit the inspiration. The CLIFF case shows that public criticism from a developer can force a costly rebrand mid-trend.
- Making a Steam-inspired game co-op/social fits Roblox's audience. Most crossover hits (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK, Meccha Chameleon) are co-op or party games.

### Gaps
- No found source documents a clone being *taken down by Roblox via DMCA* in 2025–26 with specifics.
- No quantitative data on update cadence or YouTuber coverage for specific crossover games.

## Q3. Current Roblox trend landscape (2026): genres, breakout games, algorithm, timelines

### Takeaway
Late 2026 is dominated by simulation/collect games, "steal-and-defend" games (brainrot and egg variants), roleplay, and survival/co-op horror. Survival is cited as the fastest-growing genre in October 2026. The biggest platform change was Roblox's June 2026 "Recommended For You" overhaul. It moved from 7-day to 28-day retention (split into D1, D2–7 and D8–28), replaced qualified play-through rate with more granular signals (play-through rate, a 60-second first-play bounce rate, and later "deep" play-through rate), and deliberately demotes games whose thumbnails outrun their gameplay.

Roblox exceeded 45M concurrent users in one weekend in 2025, more than all of Steam — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sim/roblox-had-more-concurrent-players-than-the-entirety-of-steam-over-the-weekend-as-it-reeled-in-over-45-million-users/)

**Algorithm and discovery (2026)**
- June 2026 Roblox newsroom post "Optimizing Discovery: How Great Games Reach Millions of Players." Overvaluing short-term engagement favoured "exciting thumbnails" over long-term value. Roblox is sharing the full signal list and relative weights with creators — [Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)

### Inferences
- Under the June 2026 algorithm, a crossover concept has to survive the first 60 seconds (low bounce) *and* bring players back over days 8–28. That favours adding a Roblox-style long-term loop (collection/index, base-building, steal-and-defend, upgrades, events) on top of the borrowed Steam mechanic. A one-session party game probably won't hold Recommended For You placement.
- The strongest genre bets for late 2026 look like survival/co-op horror (fastest growing), plus simulation/collect and steal-and-defend hybrids. Mixing a trending Steam co-op mechanic with one of these Roblox-native loops (as Dead Rails and 99 Nights did) beats a straight clone.
- Live-ops events (admin abuse/"admin war," Halloween parts) drove record peaks for Grow a Garden and are standard in Steal An Egg. A weekly event cadence appears to be expected.

## Q4. Revenue / DevEx earnings for top games of this type

### Takeaway
Platform-wide, creators earned about $1.5B through DevEx in 2025 (+63% YoY), and the top 1,000 developers averaged about $1.3M. The median DevEx cash-out is only about $1,400–$1,500 a year. Per-game revenue figures are almost all third-party estimates. The only reported per-game figure found is Grow a Garden's roughly $12M in May 2025 revenue rankings. No revenue figures were found for clones of Steam games.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 DevEx payouts were about $1.5B (+63% YoY), the first calendar year above $1B. Q4 2025 was a record $477M (+70% YoY). The top 1,000 developers averaged about $1.3M (+50% YoY). These are secondary reports of Roblox's Q4 2025 materials — [Lindsey Gamble on Threads](https://www.threads.com/@lindseygamble_/post/DUf3DVrkwJ5/in-creators-have-earned-over-billion-through-robloxs-developer-exchange-dev-ex); [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/developer-payouts-billion-dollar-economy/). Primary filing: [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)
- One analysis gives about $1.1M as the top-1,000 average instead, so the figure varies by source — [ShaneTheGamer](https://www.shanethegamer.com/research/roblox-creator-economy-analysis/)
- The median payout is about $1,440 among about 29,000 DevEx creators (year to June 2025), and about $1,500 among 42,000+ (year to June 2026) — [Panstag, Sept 2026](https://www.panstag.com/2026/09/roblox-creator-economy-earnings.html); [ZehnStudio26](https://zehn-studio26.com/news/roblox-creator-economy-payouts-2026/)

### Inferences
- Even a modest crossover hit sustaining 20K–80K CCU (like Clean the Library or the Meccha Chameleon clones) plausibly earns in the low-to-mid six figures a month if it is well monetised. This is an extrapolation from the tracker estimates above, not a reported number. The revenue curve after a trend fades (Grow a Garden going from about $12M a month to an estimated $0.5–1.2M) shows that crossover trends decay fast without retention.
- With Roblox now prioritising retention over monetisation in discovery, aggressive pay-to-win monetisation could cost both ranking and goodwill.
