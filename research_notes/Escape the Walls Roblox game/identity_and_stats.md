# Escape the Walls (Roblox): Identity, Origins and Statistics

Research date: 2026-10-26. **Method limitation:** the proxy blocked every Roblox host (games.roblox.com and apis.roblox.com returned HTTP 403 on CONNECT, and www.roblox.com was unreachable). WebFetch also failed DNS resolution for every news site tried (gamerant.com, pcgamer.com, quest-board.jp, es.kotaku.com, gamewave.fr), and the analytics sites (rolimons.com, romonitorstats.com, rotrends.com) were unreachable as well. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries, not from full page reads or live API data. **I could not retrieve any live Roblox statistic** (CCU, visits, favorites, likes, server size, maturity label).

**Key reframing for the report writer:** "Escape the Walls" is **not originally a Roblox game**. It is an unreleased indie PC game (Steam) by **Pandorio Games**. It went viral on X on 2026-10-14. The Roblox games with this name or similar names are **clones/copycats** that appeared from 2026-10-15 onward, and some of their makers reportedly said they built them with Claude. A small number of older, unrelated Roblox obbies with similar names also exist.

---

## 1. Which game is "Escape the Walls"? Place IDs, variants, and which is the main one

### Takeaway
The canonical "Escape the Walls" is Pandorio Games' upcoming Steam game (Steam app 5450560), not a Roblox experience. On Roblox, at least seven clones appeared within about a week, including one titled "Escape the Wall" / "Escape-the-Wall" that was published around 2026-10-15. I could not identify a place ID for any clone or say which clone is most popular.

### Cited Findings
- The original game is "Escape the Walls" on Steam, app ID 5450560, URL https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/. Pandorio Games is listed as developer and publisher, and the release date shows "Coming soon". — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/) (via search summary)
- Genre and premise: an incremental game in which you start with only your fists and punch through walls, break blocks, clear the rubble and escape. The Steam description mentions a 1980s atmosphere. — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/); [Pirat_Nation on X](https://x.com/Pirat_Nation/status/2111744108431569191)
- The French coverage describes the premise as breaking walls with your fists and gathering rubble to get out of a prison cell. — [Gamewave](https://gamewave.fr/roblox/roblox-pille-sans-vergogne-un-jeu-viral-en-developpement/) (dated 2026-10-19)
- Pandorio announced it with a sample video on its official X account (@pandorioltd) on 2026-10-14, described as a working title. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Pandorio on X](https://x.com/pandorioltd/status/2108549714282983605)
- A similar Roblox title, "Escape-the-Wall", was released on Roblox on 2026-10-15, the day after the announcement. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Game*Spark via Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a)
- Clones with near-identical names, such as "Escape the Wall", were already on Roblox before the Steam wishlist page went up on 2026-10-18. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/); [Nuclear Coffee (syndicated GameRant)](https://nuclearcoffee.org/viral-wall-punching-game-already-has-roblox-clones/)
- "At least seven" Roblox clones of Escape the Walls existed as of 2026-10-19. — [PC Gamer, Steam Week in Review](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/) (via search summary)
- Game*Spark reports that a clone appeared about 25 hours after the original footage: a user quote-posted Pandorio's video at 21:53 on 2026-10-15 saying "it's already on Roblox", with their own gameplay footage. — [Game*Spark via Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a); [note.com/dala47](https://note.com/dala47/n/n197f05bd1a88?hl=en)
- **Pre-existing, unrelated same-name or similar-name Roblox games:**
  - An X user claimed "there is even a Roblox obby already called Escape The Walls" before the viral moment. — [Ruben (@rdominguezibar) on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870) (unverified claim)
  - A Roblox DevForum devlog from September 2025, "Makin' It! – Escape The Walls Obby Devlog #1", describes an in-development obby. It mixes randomly generated obbies with the classic "Be Crushed By A Speeding Wall" format and uses a hub with portals. — [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/makin-it-escape-the-walls-obby-devlog-1/3930017)
  - "ESCAPE the WALL Obby!!", place ID 18873717199, credited to "The Wall Experience". Its description is mostly premium-perk promo text. This is an older game that predates the October 2026 events. — [Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/18873717199/ESCAPE-the-WALL-Obby) (search-indexed only; a second search could not re-confirm the page)
  - A YouTube playthrough is titled "Escape The Wall 🌫️ Roblox game complete Play through". It is unclear which game it shows. — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YtruEcxdRc)
- **Adjacent established Roblox games with the same mechanic:** "Punch Wall Simulator" has been on Roblox since March 2023 with "over 150 million visits", and "Wall Punch Simulator" adds "another 15 million". — [Ruben on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870) (self-reported by a social post; not independently verified)

### Inferences
- In a video or storyboard about "Escape the Walls on Roblox", the real story is most likely the **clone controversy**: a viral indie reveal, then Roblox copies within a day, then the AI-assisted "vibe-coded" cloning debate. It is unlikely to be a single dominant Roblox hit.
- Place ID 18873717199 falls in the numeric range Roblox assigned around mid-2024. If so, "ESCAPE the WALL Obby!!" predates the October 2026 clones by roughly two years. This is an inference from ID ranges, not a confirmed creation date.

### Gaps
- No place ID, universe ID, or URL was found for **any** of the October 2026 clones, including "Escape-the-Wall" (2026-10-15). Roblox's search and games APIs were blocked.
- I could not determine which same-name Roblox game is the most popular.
- I found no full list of the 7+ clones' names.

## 2. Developer: who made it, group size, other games

### Takeaway
The original is by Pandorio Games (X: @pandorioltd), a London-based studio that has mostly made mobile games and whose other PC project is the co-op physics game "Caravan Bazar". Neither the clone creators nor any Roblox group sizes were identified.

### Cited Findings
- Developer and publisher of the original: Pandorio Games. — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/)
- Pandorio is based in London. — [Game*Spark via Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a) (via Japanese search summary)
- Pandorio "has mostly made mobile games". Its current PC project is the co-op physics game **Caravan Bazar**, which has a demo on Steam. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/) (via search summary)
- Official X account: @pandorioltd. — [X](https://x.com/pandorioltd)
- Clone creators: some confirmed that they used AI, such as Claude, to build their clones. Game*Spark quoted one clone creator: "Claude did everything for me, I just sat in my chair and watched" (Japanese original: 「Claudeが全部やってくれたよ、私は椅子に座って見てただけ」). — [Game*Spark on X](https://x.com/gamespark/status/2111011804524793955); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/)
- Japanese coverage says the two clone creators it examined both appear to have used Claude. This is described as reporting-based inference. — [pixmofu](https://pixmofu.com/articles/escape-the-walls-roblox-clone-claude) (via search summary)
- Clone creators defended themselves, saying that punching walls is a common idea and calling it plagiarism is an exaggeration. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Game*Spark via Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a)

### Inferences
- A general web search for "Pandorio Games Caravan Bazar" returned nothing relevant. This suggests the studio has a small public footprint outside its Steam and X presence.

### Gaps
- No usernames or group names for the Roblox clone creators, and no group member counts.
- Pandorio's headcount, founding date and mobile catalogue are unknown.
- I found no Roblox presence for Pandorio itself.

## 3. Creation, release and update dates

### Takeaway
The original was revealed on 2026-10-14, and its Steam page went live on 2026-10-18 with no release date. The first Roblox clone appeared around 2026-10-15, and seven or more existed by 2026-10-19. Last-updated dates for the Roblox clones could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- 2026-10-14: Pandorio posted gameplay footage on X, and it went viral. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/); [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/)
- 2026-10-15: "Escape-the-Wall" was published on Roblox. At 21:53 a user posted "it's already on Roblox". — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Game*Spark via Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a)
- 2026-10-16: Game*Spark published its article (date per the Japanese search summary). — [Yahoo! Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/154aad980dc59896f171ad902f8ff34fdbb1cf0a)
- 2026-10-18: the Steam page went live for wishlisting. — [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/); [Gamewave](https://gamewave.fr/roblox/roblox-pille-sans-vergogne-un-jeu-viral-en-developpement/)
- 2026-10-19: at least 7 Roblox clones existed. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-steam-next-fest-has-over-8-000-demos-as-worthless-vibe-coded-slop-levels-up/); the Gamewave article is also dated 2026-10-19.
- Original game release date and price: not announced, and the Steam page shows "Coming soon". — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/)
- Sources conflict slightly on clone timing: "the next day" / "within 25 hours" (Game*Spark, Quest Board), "within about a week" (Gamewave summary), and "before the Steam page on Oct 18" (GameRant). These are consistent if the first clone came on 2026-10-15 and more followed through the week.

### Gaps
- Exact Roblox "Created" and "Updated" timestamps for any clone could not be retrieved, because roblox.com was blocked.

## 4. Popularity statistics (CCU, visits, favorites, likes, server size, genre, maturity)

### Takeaway
I found **no** Roblox player, visit, favorite, like/dislike, server-size or maturity-label data for any "Escape the Walls" clone. The only hard popularity numbers concern the original Steam game's wishlists and an adjacent Roblox genre benchmark.

### Cited Findings
- The original passed **500 Steam wishlists in under a day** after the page went live. The developer said the studio's previous game took about six months to reach the same number. These are self-reported figures from a Pandorio X post around 2026-10-19. — [Pandorio on X](https://x.com/pandorioltd); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/)
- Roblox genre benchmarks (not this game): Punch Wall Simulator has 150M+ visits since March 2023, and Wall Punch Simulator has about 15M. — [Ruben on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870) (unverified)
- Several search syntheses explicitly reported that they found no concurrent-player or visit data for the clones. — searches across [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/news/37989/viral-wall-punching-game-already-has-roblox-clones), [Thisisgame Thailand](https://thisisgamethailand.com/games/console-pc/pc/viral-game-escape-the-walls-copied-on-roblox-less-than-a-day/), [Ralify](https://ralify.net/stories/viral-indie-game-escape-the-walls-faces-immediate-roblox-copycat/)

### Inferences
- None of the coverage cites player numbers for the clones, which suggests none became a breakout hit large enough to be reported. Treat this as weak evidence only.

### Gaps
- All of the following are unavailable because of blocked access: CCU (current and peak), visits, favorites, likes/dislikes, max server size, Roblox genre tag, and maturity/age label for every same-name Roblox game.
- Live trackers that would hold these figures (BloxBunny, RoWatcher, RoMonitor, Rolimon's) had no indexed page for "Escape the Walls" in search results.
