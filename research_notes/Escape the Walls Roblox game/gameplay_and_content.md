# Escape the Walls (Roblox): Gameplay and Content

> **Read this first. It affects every section below.**
> 1. **No single Roblox game called "Escape the Walls" has wikis, code lists or guide coverage.** As of late October 2026, "Escape the Walls" mostly means an **unreleased indie PC game by Pandorio Games**. It went viral on X on October 14, 2026, and its Steam page went live on October 18, 2026. Within about a day, **several Roblox clones** appeared under near-identical names ("Escape the Wall", "Escape-the-Wall", "Punch Walls to Escape", "Smash Walls To Escape Prison" and others). An older, unrelated Roblox obby, **"Escape The Walls Obby [ALPHA]"** (DevForum devlogs, Sept 2025), also uses the name.

> 3. **"Most popular" could not be confirmed.** I had no visit or CCU data for any clone. ## Core loop: what are the walls, how do you escape, objective, win/lose, rounds, solo vs multiplayer?

### Takeaway
The "walls" are the breakable walls of a prison-like cell or chamber. You start with only your fists, punch the walls apart block by block, collect the rubble (sold for cash), buy upgrades so you break walls faster, and dig your way out. It is an incremental, upgrade-loop game, not a round-based survival game. The original PC game is single-player. The best-documented Roblox clone ("Punch Walls to Escape") adds co-op for up to 4 players, difficulty "sentences" and a Chapter 2.

## Levels, stages, maps, difficulty tiers, obstacles, hazards, enemies, bosses, secrets

### Takeaway
Content details exist only for "Punch Walls to Escape": tiered "sentences" (easy to hard, with the hardest unlocked after escaping on Normal), a prison-yard hub and a "Chapter 2" featuring a huge cellmate called Big Tony. The original PC game says "danger hides inside the walls" and that you go "deeper each time," but no hazards or enemies are named.

### Cited Findings
- "Punch Walls to Escape": sentences (difficulty tiers) range from easy to hard, and the hardest tier unlocks after escaping on Normal. — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/136606353774156/Punch-Walls-to-Escape) (via search summary)
- "Punch Walls to Escape" Chapter 2: "Big Tony," a huge cellmate who also wants out and needs a door big enough for him to fit through. — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/136606353774156/Punch-Walls-to-Escape)
- The original game says danger hides inside the walls, with repeat runs going deeper. — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/) (via search summary)
- The older obby namesake has randomly generated obbies, a moving crushing wall, safe-zone side branches and a boulder gate between obby groups. — [DevForum devlog #1](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/makin-it-escape-the-walls-obby-devlog-1/3930017)
- The same developer's feedback thread lists planned power-ups such as jetpacks, speed coils and invisibility cloaks for the obby. — [DevForum feedback thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/looking-for-thoughts-on-my-game/3920187) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The difficulty tiers likely change wall hardness or the number of layers. That is unconfirmed.

### Gaps

## Items, tools, upgrades, currencies, pets, skins, cosmetics, progression, rebirths

### Takeaway
"Punch Walls to Escape" is documented as follows. Currency is cash from rubble. You upgrade your fists and your vacuum, and the tool ladder runs chisel, pickaxe, jackhammer, dynamite. I found no confirmed pets, rebirths or skins for any clone. Those systems belong to older wall-punching simulators (Punch Wall Simulator, Break Wall Simulator) that should not be confused with Escape the Walls.

### Cited Findings
- Tool and upgrade progression in "Punch Walls to Escape": fists, then chisels, pickaxes, jackhammers and dynamite, plus vacuum upgrades. Rubble is vacuumed up for cash. — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/136606353774156/Punch-Walls-to-Escape) (via search summary)
- "Smash Walls To Escape Prison": train to get stronger and earn money. — [Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/107557156900608/Smash-Walls-To-Escape-Prison)
- The original PC game has dig, upgrade and automate mechanics. — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/)
- Older obby namesake: the "Tix" currency was used to unlock the next obby, and the system was later reworked. — [DevForum devlog #1](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/makin-it-escape-the-walls-obby-devlog-1/3930017)
- For contrast, these are older genre neighbours, not Escape the Walls. "Break Wall Simulator" has training for strength, rebirths, pet trading, and Premium perks of +10% Strength and +10% Punching Speed. — [Roblox: Break Wall Simulator](https://www.roblox.com/games/13013415523/Break-Wall-Simulator). Punch Wall Simulator has been on Roblox since March 2023 with 150M+ visits, and Wall Punch Simulator has about 15M. This comes from an X post and is unverified. — [Ruben on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870)

### Inferences
- Rebirths, pets and auras are standard in the "+1 Strength / Punch Wall" Roblox subgenre, so a clone may add them. I found no evidence for any specific Escape the Walls clone.
