# Escape the Walls (Roblox): Community, Reception & Competitive Landscape

Research date: 2026-10-26. **Method caveat:** in this session, direct page fetches were blocked. WebFetch failed on DNS, and the egress proxy returned 403 for roblox.com, discord.com, reddit.com, gamerant.com, store.steampowered.com, devforum.roblox.com and others. Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Numbers such as view counts should be treated as approximate. No live Roblox, Discord or Reddit data could be collected.

**Framing finding:** "Escape the Walls" is **not originally a Roblox game**. It is an in-development first-person incremental PC game (Steam) by the mobile-focused indie studio **Pandorio Games** (X: @pandorioltd). The Roblox games using this name or concept are **clones that appeared within about a day of its viral reveal on 2026-10-14**. One example is "Escape-the-Wall", released on Roblox on 2026-10-15. There is also an older, **unrelated** Roblox project, "Escape The Walls Obby". Most of the "community and reception" around the name is the clone controversy.

## Official community channels (Discord, Roblox group, X, TikTok, YouTube)

### Takeaway
The original game's official presence is the Pandorio Games X account (@pandorioltd), a Steam wishlist page (app 5450560, live 2026-10-18), and a Discord that the X account promotes. I found no official Roblox group, TikTok or YouTube channel for "Escape the Walls", and none for the Roblox clones. Member counts could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Pandorio Games announced "Escape the Walls" (working title) with a sample video on its official X account on 2026-10-14. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Game*Spark](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/16/173695.html)
- Official X posts: [pandorioltd status 2110335569255657628](https://x.com/pandorioltd/status/2110335569255657628), plus later posts [2108549714282983605](https://x.com/pandorioltd/status/2108549714282983605). The X profile is [x.com/pandorioltd](https://x.com/pandorioltd).
- The Steam page ([store.steampowered.com/app/5450560](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/)) lists Pandorio Games as developer and publisher, with release date "Coming soon" and no price. It is wishlist-only and went live on 2026-10-18. — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/); [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/escape-the-walls-roblox-clones/)

- Pandorio said the game got over 500 Steam wishlists in under a day, which it said its previous game took six months to reach. — [x.com/pandorioltd](https://x.com/pandorioltd) (per search summary; not independently verified)

- Any "official Escape the Walls Discord or Roblox group" found on Roblox is likely clone-operated, not Pandorio's. The original is a Steam title, and Pandorio is not known to have a Roblox presence.

## Creator coverage (YouTubers, streamers, TikTokers) and viral posts

### Takeaway
The viral vector was X, not YouTube or TikTok. Pandorio's reveal clip reportedly reached about 4.5M views by 2026-10-16 and was later seen at about 5.5–5.6M. Large aggregator accounts amplified the clone story, notably Pirat_Nation and the Japanese outlet Game*Spark. I found no YouTube or TikTok videos specifically about "Escape the Walls" or its Roblox clones.

### Cited Findings

## Player sentiment (Reddit, reviews, praise and complaints)

### Takeaway
No Reddit threads or player reviews were found for the Roblox versions. Public sentiment is mostly about the clone controversy. Many people sympathize with Pandorio and criticize the cloners. A cynical camp says indie devs should not reveal ideas early, and some players simply want it on Roblox. Clone makers argue the concept is generic.

### Cited Findings
- Reactions to the clone were negative, with commenters accusing the clone's creator of stealing the idea. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/)
- The clone creators argued that the concept was common and that calling it plagiarism was an exaggeration. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Kotaku en Español](https://es.kotaku.com/un-juego-indie-se-volvio-viral-antes-de-salir-en-steam-y-roblox-ya-esta-lleno-de-copias-2000055077)
- Game*Spark's headline notes voices saying "ideas shouldn't be made public" (「アイデア公開すべきではない」との声も). — [Game*Spark](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/16/173695.html)
- Pixmofu (Japanese) reportedly describes the clone creator thanking Claude, and says the original developer replied that it was a good game. This is the only source with that detail, and it conflicts with English reports that Pandorio made no official comment. — [pixmofu](https://pixmofu.com/articles/escape-the-walls-roblox-clone-claude) (per search summary)
- Rebuttal: Punch Wall Simulator has been on Roblox since March 2023 with over 150M visits, Wall Punch Simulator has about 15M more, and "There is even a Roblox obby already called Escape The Walls." The poster argues the clone "does not need to be made." — [Ruben (rdominguezibar) on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870)

### Inferences
- Gameplay-level complaints (bugs, pay-to-win, ads, difficulty) about the Roblox clones are undocumented. Clones of this kind on Roblox are typically simulator or incremental games monetized with gamepasses, but that is an assumption, not a finding.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/roblox, r/RobloxGames) could not be accessed. Search found no threads.
- No Roblox like/dislike ratios, visit counts or review text for any clone.

## Controversies (cloning, AI use, scams, drama)

### Takeaway
The defining controversy is AI-assisted cloning. At least seven Roblox clones existed by 2026-10-19, and one was live within about 24–25 hours of the reveal. Some clone creators openly credited Anthropic's Claude. The case became a widely cited example in October 2026 coverage of "vibe-coded" clones and indie developers pulling public demos. Pandorio had made no official statement as of the reports.

### Cited Findings
- A similar title, "Escape-the-Wall", was released on Roblox on 2026-10-15, the day after the 2026-10-14 announcement. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/)

### Gaps

- I could not confirm the pixmofu claim that Pandorio replied "it's a good game".

## Similar or copycat games, and the genre the game fits

### Takeaway
The Roblox "Escape the Walls" games sit within an established Roblox wall-punching simulator/incremental niche that predates Pandorio's game. Punch Wall Simulator (since March 2023, 150M+ visits) and Wall Punch Simulator (about 15M visits) are the main examples. A separate "Escape The Walls Obby" also exists, built on the classic "Be Crushed By A Speeding Wall" formula. Pandorio's game itself is a first-person incremental "punch out of a prison cell" game.

### Cited Findings
- Pandorio's game is an incremental game: smash walls with bare hands and clear or collect rubble to escape a prison-like chamber. — [Quest Board.JP](https://quest-board.jp/en/quests/escape-the-walls/); [Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/10/19/192648); [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5450560/Escape_the_Walls/)
- Punch Wall Simulator has been on Roblox since March 2023 with over 150M visits, and Wall Punch Simulator has about 15M. — [Ruben on X](https://x.com/rdominguezibar/status/2110794822550474870) (third-party claim, not verified against Roblox)
- "Escape The Walls Obby" (Roblox DevForum devlog) combines "randomly generated obbies and the classic Be Crushed By A Speeding Wall game". Walls were enlarged from 40x40 to 100x100 studs, and a portal hub was added because players left after the first obby. — [DevForum: Makin' It! - Escape The Walls Obby Devlog #1](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/makin-it-escape-the-walls-obby-devlog-1/3930017)
- Wall-punching or breaking videos are a long-running Roblox YouTube genre, for example "Breaking 987,345,123 WALLS With ONE PUNCH In Roblox..." and "Punch Walls to Save Brainrots In Roblox". — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pOIErS3_Hkk); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OYpmEWXk4m4)

### Inferences
- The Roblox clones blend Pandorio's "escape the cell" framing and name with the existing Roblox punch-simulator loop: punch, gain strength, break bigger walls. That loop also fits the 2025–26 "brainrot" and incremental simulator trend.
- The "Tsunami", "Escape Running Head" and "Run from the wall" titles in the brief are a different subgenre (escape a moving threat). Only the "Escape The Walls Obby" lineage, via Be Crushed By A Speeding Wall, overlaps with it.

### Gaps
- No verified visit or CCU numbers for any "Escape the Wall(s)" Roblox clone, Punch Wall Simulator, or the obby, because Roblox hosts were blocked.
- Comparative quality and monetization data for the clones is unavailable.
